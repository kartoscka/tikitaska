# Feature table: data dictionary

Generated by `python -m src.features --dictionary-only` from the column spec in that file. Do not edit by hand; edit `COLUMNS` / `CONSTANTS` and regenerate. Contains no data values.

Table: `reports/features.parquet` (gitignored, local only). One row per tactical video frame, `frame` 0 .. 199318, same index as the submission.

Build: `python -m src.features`

## Coordinates

- Metres. `x` = ATD normalised x * 105.3 m (pitch length, `games.py`), `y` = ATD normalised y * 68.0 m (pitch width).
- Origin = the pitch corner at ATD (0, 0). `x` runs along the touchlines from one goal line (x = 0) to the other (x = 105.3); `y` runs along the goal lines from one touchline (y = 0) to the other (y = 68.0).
- Attacking direction is not fixed: use `h_attacks_pos_x` (derived per half) or `ball_x_att_h`.
- Times: 25 frames per second. Velocities m/s, accelerations m/s^2, angles radians.

## Sources and what is not used

- ATD raw text (players and ball, `src/features.load_atd`), ATD XML (slot teams, `starter/baseline.team_of_each_track`), homography (`starter/load_homography`), Smart Tagging (`starter/load_smart_tagging`).
- Not used: the ATD `speed_kph` columns (they match neither km/h nor m/s), the public GT (never read, not for features, not for thresholds), the broadcast recording.

## Constants

| name | value | unit | reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `BALL_MAX_SPEED_MS` | 50.0 | m/s | The hardest shots reach about 35-40 m/s. Anything faster between two observations is a tracker error, not the ball. |
| `PLAYER_MAX_SPEED_MS` | 12.5 | m/s | Top sprinters peak around 10-11 m/s. Faster means the slot switched to another person or a false detection. |
| `BALL_POS_TOL_M` | 1.0 | m | Slack added to every ball jump test. The ATD ball has no height and its dot sits behind or beside the real ball by up to about a metre (seen on the video), so that much is noise, not a jump. |
| `PLAYER_POS_TOL_M` | 0.5 | m | Slack added to every player jump test: the foot point of a detection box wobbles by about half a metre when legs or neighbours overlap. |
| `MIN_PIECE_FRAMES` | 5 | frames | 0.2 s. A stretch of a track that is reached and left by physically impossible jumps, is shorter than this, and whose neighbours line up again across it, is a short swap to the wrong object (a real ball or player cannot teleport away and back). It is dropped. |
| `BALL_INTERP_MAX_FRAMES` | 25 | frames | 1 s. Within about a second a ball in play keeps its path (a pass or a dribble), so a straight line between the last and next sightings is a fair guess. Longer gaps can hide a touch, a deflection or a stoppage, so they stay missing. |
| `BALL_FIT_HALF` | 4 | frames | Half-width of the local straight-line fit for ball position and velocity: 9 frames, 0.36 s. Long enough to average the per-frame detection noise, short enough that a pass (usually longer than 0.3 s) stays a separate movement. |
| `PLAYER_FIT_HALF` | 6 | frames | Half-width of the local straight-line fit for player position and velocity: 13 frames, 0.52 s. Players change velocity more slowly than the ball, so a wider window is safe. |
| `BALL_TURN_LAG` | 4 | frames | ball_turn compares the ball velocity 4 frames (0.16 s) before and after a frame: about the time a touch takes to show up in the fitted velocity. |
| `BALL_TURN_MIN_SPEED_MS` | 2.0 | m/s | Below walking pace the direction of the fitted velocity is mostly noise, so ball_turn is left empty. |
| `BALL_OUT_MARGIN_M` | 1.0 | m | The ball is out only when all of it is over the line (radius 0.11 m) and the ATD position is noisy by up to about a metre, so the ball must be this far past a line to count as out. |
| `CONTROL_RADIUS_M` | 2.0 | m | A player controlling the ball has it within a stride (about 1-1.5 m); plus position noise. |
| `PRESSURE_RADIUS_M` | 5.0 | m | Usual distance at which a defender counts as pressing the ball carrier. |
| `IMAGE_SIZE` | (1920, 1080) | pixels | Size of the tactical video the homography maps into. A player projected outside it is off camera. |
| `MAX_CLEAN_PASSES` | 50 | passes | Safety cap on the jump-removal loop (it normally stops after a few passes). Not a tuning knob. |

## Cleaning notes

- **Jumps** (ball and every player slot): consecutive observations further apart than max speed x elapsed time + tolerance split a track into pieces. Short pieces whose neighbours line up across them are dropped; a short piece at either end is dropped when its only neighbour is long. Long wrong-object pieces cannot be told apart from real movement and are kept; velocities never cross a piece boundary.
- **Ball gaps**: only gaps up to `BALL_INTERP_MAX_FRAMES` inside one piece are filled (`ball_interp`). Players are not interpolated.
- **Smoothing / velocity**: centred straight-line fit (local linear regression) over the cleaned points of the same piece; level = smoothed position, slope = velocity. Acceleration = the same fit applied to the velocity.
- **Off-camera players** are kept (the tracker extrapolates them) but counted in `{t}_n_offcam` and flagged on the nearest player (`{t}_nearest_offcam`).

## Known defects

- The ATD ball has no height: aerial balls are projected to the ground and can look fast or jumpy. Never trust a single frame.
- The ATD ball position is noisy, sometimes behind or beside the real ball.
- Off-camera players are unreliable; a keeper slot can sit on the wrong player.
- Slots are tracklets, not people: `{t}_nearest_slot` can change while the same player keeps the ball.
- Smart Tagging is automatic (false positives and negatives). Team tag meaning is confirmed only for DEFENDING codes (defending side). Throw-ins and kick-offs are always N/A.
